Privacy, simply.

GridZero does not collect or transmit your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or in-app purchases.

On your device

Your puzzle progress, notes, recent puzzle IDs, elapsed time, and settings stay in the app's local storage. GridZero works offline, including puzzle generation. Deleting the app removes its local data; Apple's device backup and restore features may retain or restore app data according to your device settings.

The Android app requests no network permission and requires no Google services. Its backup and device-transfer rules exclude local game data and settings.

Scheduled light and dark appearance uses your device's clock. It does not use your location.

On this website

This site uses no JavaScript, cookies, analytics, embedded third-party media, or tracking fonts. It is served by Cloudflare, which processes ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for its practices.

Links and beta testing

If you follow a link to GitHub or Apple, their services have their own privacy policies. Apple may process TestFlight installation information, feedback, or crash reports through its beta-testing service. GridZero includes no separate reporting or analytics service.

Questions

Visit Support. If you choose to post an issue on GitHub, the information you include is handled by GitHub and may be public.
